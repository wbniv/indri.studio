v0.8 · Progress resets when you restart

Keyboard: arrows or I/J/K/L move. Space selects/interacts and continues the reader. 2 switches camera; hold 2 for the alternate camera. 4 toggles reduced motion. 3 or Left closes the reader; Up/Down changes text size.

Gamepad: D-pad/left stick moves, A selects, B changes camera, X closes. Click or tap the game to resume after using the toolbar. Press Escape to leave fullscreen or release keyboard focus.

On phones, Play opens fullscreen when supported. Touch controls are experimental. Increase text size with Up if the reader is small; browser/device compatibility is still being verified.
