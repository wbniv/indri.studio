Keyboard: arrows or I/J/K/L move. Space selects/interacts and continues the reader. 2 switches camera; hold 2 for the alternate camera. 3 or Left closes dialogue; Up/Down changes text size. 4 toggles reduced motion.

Joystick: drag to move or turn; release to stop. Use another finger to hold Select or another action.

Gamepad: D-pad/left stick moves, A selects, B changes camera, X closes. Gamepad and phone support are experimental.

Reduced motion quiets decorative animations and makes some visual transitions immediate. Walking and story choices remain available. Escape closes this menu first, then exits fullscreen or releases game focus.
